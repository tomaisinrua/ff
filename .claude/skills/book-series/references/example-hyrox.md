## Slide 2/12 — 1. The Discovery

> Sebastian has been running for fifteen years.
> One afternoon, someone at parkrun mentions HYROX.
> Apparently, it involves running and lifting heavy things.
> Sebastian thinks this sounds ridiculous.
> By teatime, he has entered three events.

**Caption:** One innocent conversation at parkrun. Three race entries before teatime. Sebastian has found his new purpose. 😂

---

## Slide 3/12 — 2. The New Vocabulary

> Sebastian no longer goes to the gym.
> He does functional fitness.
> He talks about SkiErgs, compromised running and wall-ball efficiency.
> Camilla asks whether he is still speaking English.
> Sebastian explains that she wouldn't understand.
> She agrees.

**Caption:** He used to go to the gym. Now he optimises compromised running and wall-ball efficiency. Camilla would just like a normal conversation.

---

## Slide 4/12 — 3. The Sled Push

> Sebastian has discovered the sled push.
> He spends forty minutes pushing a large metal object across the gym.
> He pays £65 a month for the privilege.
> Camilla suggests moving the wardrobe upstairs.
> Sebastian explains that this is completely different.

**Caption:** £65 a month to push heavy furniture around. Yet the wardrobe upstairs remains untouched. 💸😂

---

## Slide 5/12 — 4. The Missing Shirt

> Sebastian used to run in a T-shirt.
> He now trains without one.
> He says this helps regulate his body temperature.
> Camilla points out that it is February.
> Sebastian explains that serious athletes run hot.
> She asks him to turn the heating down.

**Caption:** It's February. Everyone else is wearing layers. Sebastian has decided shirts are holding back his athletic potential. 🥶💪

---

## Slide 6/12 — 5. The Headband

> Sebastian has purchased six headbands.
> He has one for training, one for racing and four for different levels of intensity.
> Camilla asks why he needs so many.
> Sebastian explains that sweat management is crucial to performance.
> Camilla points out that he has only been exercising for eleven minutes.

**Caption:** Six headbands. Eleven minutes of exercise. Sweat management is apparently a very complicated science. 😂

---

## Slide 7/12 — 6. The Screaming

> Sebastian has learned that HYROX requires encouragement.
> He now shouts during every exercise.
> He screams at the sled, the rowing machine and occasionally himself.
> A woman asks whether he is injured.
> Sebastian explains that he is finding another level.
> She asks him to find it more quietly.

**Caption:** Some people train quietly. Sebastian prefers to announce every repetition to the entire postcode. 📢🤣

---

## Slide 8/12 — 7. The Training Video

> Sebastian films every workout.
> He records his warm-up, his sled push and himself lying on the floor afterwards.
> He adds dramatic music and the words NO EXCUSES.
> Camilla watches the finished video.
> She asks whether he managed to do any exercise between filming.

**Caption:** Forty-five minutes at the gym. Thirty-eight minutes filming. Seven minutes exercising. NO EXCUSES. 📱😂

---

## Slide 9/12 — 8. The Doubles Partner

> Sebastian has entered mixed doubles.
> He tells his partner they must communicate perfectly.
> He has prepared a twelve-page race strategy.
> She asks whether he could simply run a bit faster.
> Sebastian explains that it is much more complicated.
> She is beginning to regret agreeing.

**Caption:** Twelve pages of race strategy. Colour-coded transitions. Detailed pacing charts. His partner just wants him to run faster. 🤦‍♀️

---

## Slide 10/12 — 9. The Race Day Transformation ⭐ *the warmth beat*

> Sebastian arrives at his first HYROX.
> There are flashing lights, loud music and hundreds of people shouting.
> He removes his shirt and adjusts his headband.
> He screams before pushing the sled.
> He screams again after finishing.
> **Camilla has never seen him so happy.**

**Caption:** The lights. The music. The screaming. The shirtless celebrations. Sebastian has finally found somewhere he belongs. 🏆😂

---

## Slide 11/12 — 10. The New Identity

> Sebastian has completed his first HYROX.
> He has bought the photographs, ordered another headband and entered four more races.
> Camilla asks whether he still considers himself a runner.
> Sebastian looks offended.
> He explains that he is now a hybrid athlete.
> She asks whether that means he can finally help with the shopping.

**Caption:** He's no longer just a runner. He's a HYBRID ATHLETE. Camilla is delighted. The weekly shopping might finally get carried in. 🛍️🤣

---

## Slide 12/12 — 11. The Next Obsession

> Sebastian is already planning his next HYROX.
> He has entered races in London, Manchester, Paris and New York.
> He researches hotels, flights and optimal race nutrition.
> Camilla asks whether he is going on holiday or competing in the Olympics.
> Sebastian explains that it is a global HYROX tour.
> She suggests a weekend in a quiet cottage instead.
> Sebastian says there is no rest for a hybrid athlete.
>
> **THE END OF VOLUME 1**
> *Sebastian has found his new identity. Unfortunately, this is only the beginning.*

**Caption:** London. Manchester. Paris. New York. Sebastian is planning a world tour. Camilla is planning an escape. 🌍😂
