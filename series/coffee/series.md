**Cast:** Hugo (Enthusiast) · Davina (Observer)

### Running visual gag

The kitchen deteriorates across the series. Slide 2: a normal kitchen with a
kettle. Slide 12: every surface lost to equipment, the kettle nowhere, Davina
microwaving a mug of tea in the background. Build it gradually — one new
machine per slide.

### Locked character sentences

Paste these **verbatim** into all twelve image prompts. Do not paraphrase.

- **HUGO:** *Hugo, a lean man in his forties with round tortoiseshell glasses, a closely cropped beard and a canvas apron*
- **DAVINA:** *Davina, a woman with dark shoulder-length hair in a grey roll-neck jumper and jeans*
- **PAPER:** *heavily foxed cream paper with brown water-staining, torn crumbling edges and visible paper grain*

### Branding note

Specialty coffee has no single wordmark, so the over-branding gag adapts: every
surface carries **400g roastery bags in a dozen different pastel colours**, each
with a different farm name. Ask for at least six visible bags per scene, plus
logo-stamped glassware, tampers, aprons and tote bags.

### Localisation note

Chapter 4 says *Dublin water*. Swap for your audience — London, Manchester,
Edinburgh. Keep it a named city; "the local water" is not funny.
