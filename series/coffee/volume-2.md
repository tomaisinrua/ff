# WHEN A MAN WHO DRANK INSTANT DISCOVERS SPECIALTY COFFEE
### Volume 2 — The Situation Worsens

**Cast:** Hugo (Enthusiast) · Davina (Observer) — unchanged from Volume 1.

Volume 1 ended with Davina suggesting a week in Portugal, and Hugo observing
that Portugal has excellent roasters. Volume 2 opens on the return flight. It
does not reset. Nothing has calmed down.

### Running visual gag — Volume 2

Volume 1's gag was the kitchen deteriorating one machine per slide. Volume 2
escalates it to the **whole house, room by room**, and Davina retreats ahead of
it: garage (3), under the sink (2), the freezer (5), the spare room (7), the
kitchen table (8). By slide 12 she has been pushed out of frame entirely — the
final illustration should find her in a different room altogether.

### Locked character sentences

Identical to Volume 1. Paste **verbatim** into all twelve prompts — this is what
keeps two volumes looking like one series.

- **HUGO:** *Hugo, a lean man in his forties with round tortoiseshell glasses, a closely cropped beard and a canvas apron*
- **DAVINA:** *Davina, a woman with dark shoulder-length hair in a grey roll-neck jumper and jeans*
- **PAPER:** *heavily foxed cream paper with brown water-staining, torn crumbling edges and visible paper grain*

Volume 2 only: Hugo's apron is now **stained and scorched**, and there is a
**roasting thermometer in the chest pocket**. Add that clause everywhere, but
change nothing else.
